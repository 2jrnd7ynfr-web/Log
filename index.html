Doses, limits & data

Reminders arrive as Claude app notifications on your phone when a dose next becomes available, and stay silent between 10 pm and 7 am.

The pre-filled doses, minimum gaps and 24-hour limits are typical adult figures — set them to match your prescription and follow your surgeon's or pharmacist's directions, not this page. The paracetamol total must include any combination products (e.g. co-codamol, cold & flu remedies). This log is a memory aid, not medical advice.
